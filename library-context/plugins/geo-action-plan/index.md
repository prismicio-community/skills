## How it works

This plugin installs one skill, **GEO Action Plan Skill** (`geo-action-plan`). Point it at a prompt you track in your AI visibility tool: it clusters every page AI assistants cite and every page that ranks for their follow-up searches, checks each topic against your site, and delivers a ranked workbook of pages to create or update, plus the third-party sites and threads worth getting mentioned in.

See the skill's page for the full guide: how it works, setup, personalization, what it isn't, and pitfalls.

## Setup

Connect your AI visibility tool. Its connector must provide your tracked prompts and the pages AI assistants cite for them; Peec AI is recommended because it also provides fan-out queries. Web search and code execution must be on. Then ask the assistant to "personalize the GEO action plan skill for my brand".
