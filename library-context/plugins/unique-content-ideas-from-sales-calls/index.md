## How it works

This plugin installs one skill, **Unique Content Ideas from Sales Calls Skill** (`unique-content-ideas-from-sales-calls`). Point it at a week of recorded sales and customer calls. It reads every transcript in full, pulls out what customers actually do and decide, and checks each finding against what's already published. Only the findings nobody else has written about survive, each with the customer's own words as evidence and a set of article ideas.

See the skill's page for the full guide: how it works, setup, personalization, what it isn't, and pitfalls.

## Setup

Connect your call recorder (Gong, Modjo, Fireflies, Fathom, Avoma, Chorus or similar), or have transcripts ready to upload. Web search must be on. Then ask the assistant to "personalize the unique content ideas skill for my brand".
